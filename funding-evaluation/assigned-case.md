## The bet

Meridian builds an AI-assisted estimation layer into the Standard tier, reducing time-to-quote for project managers by 20%.

## Case inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Standard-to-Enterprise upsell rate, baseline | 6% |
| Target upsell rate | 8% within two quarters |
| Standard accounts | 400 |
| Average Enterprise contract | $28,000/year |
| Annual Enterprise churn | 12% |
| Standard CAC | $620 |
| Feature build cost | $180,000 |

## Return claimed by the case

- Each upsell generates $28,000 in incremental ARR.
- At 8% across 400 Standard accounts: 32 Enterprise upsells in Year 1.
- Claimed incremental ARR: $896,000.
- Claimed feature-build payback: 2.4 months after launch.

These are the case's claims, not validated investment conclusions. See [the evaluation](evaluation-and-funding-decision.md) for baseline adjustment and missing assumptions.

## Stated kill criterion

If Standard-to-Enterprise upsell has not reached 7% by the end of Q3, pause the AI estimation feature and reallocate Q4 engineering capacity before headcount is committed.

## Missing inputs to resolve

- Standard-tier annual contract value and whether the stated $28,000 is genuinely net expansion revenue rather than the full Enterprise contract price.
- Why an AI capability included in Standard would cause customers to upgrade to Enterprise.
- Evidence that the feature causes the upsell lift rather than sales activity or other changes.
- Comparable measurement windows for the 6% baseline, two-quarter target, Q3 kill threshold, and Year 1 return.
- Upgrade timing, gross margin, AI inference/support costs, and retention assumptions for the incremental cohort.
- Named owner and cohort/denominator definition for the Q3 stop decision.
